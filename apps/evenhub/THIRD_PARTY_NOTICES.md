# Even font measurements

@evenrealities/pretext 0.1.4 supplies firmware glyph metrics for single-line labels.
Its published package metadata declares the MIT license; the package contains no separate copyright notice.
Source: https://www.npmjs.com/package/@evenrealities/pretext
The distributed app includes `licenses/Even-Pretext-MIT.txt`. Measurements are local and make no network requests.
The build losslessly packs its glyph/range/kerning tables; upstream measurement functions and font coverage are preserved.
